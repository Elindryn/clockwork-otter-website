### The focus-ring gap you need to resolve (not just copy in)

`CLOCKWORK-BRAND1` added a two-tone halo focus ring for primary buttons (white inner ring + Charcoal outer ring), because Steel Blue — the design system's default focus color — is nearly invisible against the new copper button fill (1.08:1). **Its contrast math for the Charcoal outer ring only checked it against Foundry Paper (13.2:1, passes).** It was never checked against Foundry Charcoal itself — and the site's single most important primary button, the hero's "Explore Antiphon" CTA, sits directly on a Foundry Charcoal background. A Charcoal ring against a Charcoal section is approximately 1:1 — effectively invisible.

You need to resolve this for real, not implement the halo as documented and move on. Options include (not exhaustive):
- An outer-ring color that adapts to the section background (White or Foundry Paper when the button sits on Charcoal; Charcoal when it sits on a light section) — the CTA appears in exactly two contexts on this site after this phase (hero, on Charcoal; the Antiphon-intro section on Home and the Antiphon page itself, both presumably light — confirm background colors as you build those sections in Scope items 3–4).
- A single ring treatment that works against both without switching — check whether one exists before assuming it doesn't (re-derive the math yourself; don't trust the "no single color works" conclusion from the Brand-1 result without independently checking it, the same discipline this project has applied to prior contrast claims).

Show your math. State clearly which contexts you tested and what you changed.
